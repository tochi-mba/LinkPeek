INSTALLATION

Install in four steps.

Download LinkPeek.crx, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and drag the downloaded file onto that page. Approve the prompt. Opening the CRX directly can cause Chromium to reject a self-hosted package.

CORE CONTROLS

Everything important is under one hand.

Hover

Open a stable preview after the intent delay. Move into the panel without losing it.

Scroll or ↑ ↓

Move through media. In grid view, vertical scrolling moves through the grid normally.

Horizontal scroll

Scrub faster through long galleries. Direction and sensitivity are configurable.

Pinch or double-click

Zoom around the pointer. Scroll pans while an image is zoomed.

Grid and pin

Use the visible controls or press G for grid and P to hold the current preview.

Move and resize

Drag the header and pull any edge or corner. Your normal panel layout is remembered; double-click its title to reset it.

PERFORMANCE

Choose how early LinkPeek prepares media.

Balanced

Warms likely nearby links and a few preview thumbnails with shared request and memory limits.

Data saver

Disables speculative destination loading and keeps only a minimal preview ring.

Fast

Warms more visible links and nearby thumbnails, while still avoiding original files and unbounded galleries.

EMPTY PAGES

Search one level deeper.

Linked-page Fallback can inspect links only when the opened page has no qualifying media. Same site is the recommended mode. Depth, page, and total-media caps prevent loops and runaway galleries; failed or blocked child pages are skipped.

LINK FILTER

Activate only where it matters.

Add words or phrases under Hover & Activation → Activation Keywords. When the list is not empty, LinkPeek only activates or preloads destination URLs containing at least one entry. Matching is case-insensitive; commas and new lines separate entries.

TROUBLESHOOTING

When a preview is still empty.

The destination may block cross-origin requests, require a signed-in session, contain no qualifying media, or use a layout the generic extractor cannot identify. Try opening the source, lowering media-size filters, or pausing LinkPeek on that site. Clear the cache after changing extraction rules.
