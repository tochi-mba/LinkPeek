PRIVACY

Your previews stay in your browser.

LinkPeek has no account system, analytics, advertising, or LinkPeek backend. Balanced mode may request a small number of nearby destinations before you hover so their previews are ready. Those requests go directly to the destination and its media host. You can turn prefetching off.

Network

Balanced mode limits concurrent work and warms up to three thumbnails for a selected nearby link. Data saver disables speculative loading.

Storage

Preferences and saved-link metadata live in browser extension storage. Scan and GIF caches are held in memory and use configured limits.

Permissions

Host access fetches preview destinations, storage saves preferences, and downloads supports explicit original-media downloads.

LinkPeek does not send destination URLs, settings, scan results, or browsing history to REX Technologies.
