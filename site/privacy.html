PRIVACY

Your previews stay in your browser.

LinkPeek has no account system, analytics service, advertising service, or LinkPeek backend. When you intentionally preview or prefetch a link, the extension requests that destination directly so it can identify media. Settings and site profiles are stored in browser extension storage.

Network

Requests go to the pages/media hosts needed for the preview. Prefetching can be disabled completely.

Storage

Preferences and site profiles live locally. Scan results are held in an in-memory cache for the configured lifetime.

Permissions

Host access is required to fetch hovered destinations; storage saves preferences; downloads supports explicit original-media downloads.
